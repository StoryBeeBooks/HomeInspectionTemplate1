Meet Michael Heart, RHI

Registered Home Inspector & Founder of TO Inspect Ltd.

With years of hands-on experience in construction and renovation, Michael brings a unique perspective to every inspection. As a Registered Home Inspector (RHI) and proud member of the Ontario Association of Home Inspectors (OAHI), he is committed to providing thorough, unbiased assessments that empower you to make confident real estate decisions.

Michael is recognized as a Tarion warranty expert, having conducted hundreds of Pre-Delivery Inspections (PDIs) for new home buyers across the GTA. His philosophy is simple: an informed homeowner is an empowered homeowner.
