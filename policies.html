Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 29, 2025

At Toronto Home Inspection Corp. ("we," "us," or "our"), we are committed to maintaining the accuracy, confidentiality, and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes the personal information that we collect from or about you, how we use and to whom we disclose that information, and how you can correct or access it.

This policy has been developed in compliance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation in Ontario.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that is necessary to provide our home inspection services and run our business. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and current mailing address.
  • Property Information: Address of the property to be inspected, access details (lockbox codes), and specific concerns regarding the property.
  • Transaction Information: Services purchased, pricing, and payment details.
  • Real Estate Professional Information: Name and contact details of the Real Estate Agent representing you (if applicable).
Note: We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers; payments are processed through secure third-party payment gateways (e.g., Stripe, Square).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To schedule and perform home inspection services.
  • To generate and deliver the Inspection Report.
  • To communicate with you regarding appointments, quotes, and follow-up questions.
  • To process payments and issue receipts.
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud.
  • To comply with legal, insurance, and regulatory requirements (including tax reporting).

3. Disclosure of Information

We value your trust. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may disclose your personal information in the following limited circumstances:

  • To Your Realtor: We may share the Inspection Report or summary with your Real Estate Agent only with your verbal or written consent.
  • Service Providers: We may transfer data to trusted third-party service providers who assist us in running our business (e.g., report-writing software companies like Horizon/Spectora, payment processors, or cloud storage providers). These providers are contractually bound to protect your information.
  • Legal & Insurance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to our insurance providers in the event of a liability claim.
  • The Seller: We do not share the inspection report with the seller or the seller's agent without your explicit permission.

4. Storage and Retention

Your personal information and Inspection Reports are stored electronically on secure servers.

  • Retention Period: We retain your personal information and inspection reports for a minimum period of seven (7) years to comply with Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) requirements and professional liability (insurance) standards.
  • After the retention period expires, data is securely destroyed or anonymized.

5. Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data against loss, theft, and unauthorized access. This includes:

  • Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption on our website.
  • Password-protected software and devices.
  • Restricted access to personal information (only authorized personnel have access).

6. Cookies and Website Data

When you visit our website, we may use "cookies" to analyze web traffic and improve your experience. These cookies do not collect personally identifiable information. You can choose to decline cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect your ability to use certain features of our site. See our Cookie Policy for more details.

7. Access and Correction

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to request corrections if you believe it is inaccurate. To make a request, please contact our Privacy Officer at the email listed below.

8. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."

Accessibility Policy (AODA)

Effective Date: November 29, 2025

1. Our Commitment

Toronto Home Inspection Corp. is committed to treating all people in a way that allows them to maintain their dignity and independence. We believe in integration and equal opportunity. We are committed to meeting the needs of people with disabilities in a timely manner and will do so by preventing and removing barriers to accessibility and meeting accessibility requirements under the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA).

2. Communication

We will communicate with people with disabilities in ways that take into account their disability.

  • We will train staff who communicate with customers on how to interact and communicate with people with various types of disabilities.
  • Upon request, we will provide inspection reports or invoices in alternative formats (e.g., Large Print or text-only for screen readers).

3. Assistive Devices

We are committed to serving people with disabilities who use assistive devices to obtain, use, or benefit from our services. We ensure that our staff are trained and familiar with various assistive devices that may be used by customers while accessing our services.

Note Regarding Inspection Sites:
While we strive to make our service accessible, please note that as Home Inspectors, we inspect third-party private properties (the homes being sold). We do not control the accessibility of these private dwellings (e.g., lack of ramps, narrow doorways). However, we will make every reasonable effort to accommodate clients, such as reviewing photos/videos of inaccessible areas (like attics or basements) with the client in an accessible area of the home or virtually.

4. Service Animals

We welcome people with disabilities and their service animals. Service animals are allowed on the parts of our premises that are open to the public.

  • When conducting an inspection at a private residence, we will respect the homeowner's property while advocating for the client's right to be accompanied by their service animal, provided it is safe to do so.

5. Support Persons

A person with a disability who is accompanied by a support person will be allowed to have that person accompany them on our premises or during the inspection.

  • Fees: No admission fee or additional surcharge will be charged for support persons attending a home inspection.

6. Notice of Temporary Disruption

In the event of a planned or unexpected disruption to services or facilities for customers with disabilities (e.g., if our website or report download portal is down), Toronto Home Inspection Corp. will notify customers promptly. This clearly posted notice will include information about the reason for the disruption, its anticipated length of time, and a description of alternative facilities or services, if available.

7. Training

Toronto Home Inspection Corp. will provide training to employees, volunteers, and others who deal with the public or other third parties on our behalf. Training will include:

  • An overview of the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 and the requirements of the customer service standard.
  • Toronto Home Inspection Corp.'s plan related to the customer service standard.
  • How to interact and communicate with people with various types of disabilities.
  • How to interact with people with disabilities who use an assistive device or require the assistance of a service animal or a support person.
  • What to do if a person with a disability is having difficulty in accessing our services.

8. Feedback Process

Customers who wish to provide feedback on the way Toronto Home Inspection Corp. provides goods and services to people with disabilities can contact us via:

  • Email: mike@homeinspection.ca
  • Phone: Contact us directly

All feedback, including complaints, will be directed to Mike. Customers can expect to hear back within 5 business days.

9. Modifications to This or Other Policies

Any policy of Toronto Home Inspection Corp. that does not respect and promote the dignity and independence of people with disabilities will be modified or removed.
